## ⚔️ The Art of the Clash (Combat System)

Experience a unique **Simultaneous Combat System**. Unlike traditional turn-based games, in Easy Hit, cards "clash" at the same time.

### 🛡️ Defense as a Shield
Every point of **DEF (Defense)** acts as a physical shield. 
* Incoming **ATQ (Attack)** damage is first mitigated by your DEF.
* Once your armor is shattered (DEF reaches 0), the remaining damage strikes your **HP (Health)** directly.
* **Victory Condition:** The first card to have its HP reduced to zero loses the duel.

### 🧬 Elements & Classes
Strategy starts before the first hit. Every card belongs to an **Element** and a **Class**, creating a complex web of tactical advantages:
* **Elements:** Fire, Water, Nature, Lightning, Wind, Light, and Darkness.
* **Classes:** Robot, Dragon, Human, Spectre, or Neutral.

### 🧪 The 7400 Balancing Rule
To maintain a competitive ecosystem, every card must be forged under the **7400 Point Limit**. Distribute your points wisely between ATQ, DEF, and HP to create the ultimate warrior without breaking the balance.
